# DEVIATION

<img src="/assets/game_art/banner3.png">

Ever feel like you’re just... normal? Finally, there’s a trophy for that! DEVIATION is a social psychology game for friends who think they know each other. Everyone enters a secret number, but the winner isn't the highest or the lowest—it's the person who pins down the group's average.

It’s not just a game of math; it’s a game of manipulation. Do you play it safe with a "normal" number, or do you try to sabotage the results by going high to skew the mean? You’ll have to get inside your friends' heads: are they thinking logically, or are they trying to be the outlier? Every round is a fresh experiment in groupthink, ego, and the hilarious reality of how "average" your friends actually are.

Watch the results graph reveal your friends' chaotic logic in real-time. Did someone try to sabotage the mean? Did everyone think the same way? Grab your friends and find out who is the most average person in the room!
